Health Information

Accurate Health Information for Young Parents in Tororo District

Hope 4 Teen Parents Uganda provides age-appropriate, accurate and confidential health education to adolescents, teenage mothers, teenage fathers and their children. All information follows Ugandan clinical guidelines and is delivered without shame or judgment.

How We Work

How H4TPU Delivers Health Information

Health information reaches young parents through multiple channels — not just one.

🏘️

Community Outreach

Quarterly outreach sessions bring health education directly into communities across Tororo District, reaching young people where they live.

👥

Group Education Sessions

Scheduled youth service days and teenage parent support group sessions cover health topics in a safe, non-judgmental group setting.

🤝

Individual Consultations

Young parents can access private, confidential consultations to ask questions and receive personal guidance without fear of judgment.

🎓

Peer Education

Trained youth peer educators share approved health information among their peers, counter misinformation and encourage service use.
