Support Our Work

Help Fund a 36-Month Community Health Programme for Young Parents.

Hope 4 Teen Parents Uganda is implementing a structured health programme for adolescents, teenage mothers, teenage fathers and their children in Tororo District. We are seeking strategic partnerships, financial support, technical assistance, commodities and equipment to make it happen.

Why Your Support Matters

Behind Every Donation Is a Young Parent Who Gets Help.

Right now, there are teenage mothers in Mella Trading Center and surrounding communities who are pregnant, frightened, and have no trusted person to ask for health information. There are young fathers who want to step up but don't know how.

Your support — in any form — changes that. It funds community sessions, health education materials, outreach, referrals and the people who show up for young parents every single day.

Health worker connections & referralsLinking young parents to qualified health workers and appropriate health facilities

Partnership & Support

Other Ways to Support H4TPU

The programme explicitly seeks the following types of partner support — beyond cash donations.

🙋

Volunteer

Health workers, educators, counsellors and community members can volunteer their time and skills to deliver programme activities.

Volunteer With Us
🏥

Partner Organisation

Government health structures, NGOs, schools, faith-based organisations, labs and pharmacies are all invited to partner with H4TPU for referral and service delivery.

Become a Partner
📦

Supplies & Equipment

We welcome in-kind donations of medical supplies, health education materials, outreach equipment, printing support and data tools.

Donate Supplies
🎓

Technical Assistance

Development partners and NGOs can support H4TPU through technical assistance in clinical quality, safeguarding, M&E, data systems and staff training.

Offer Support

From the official H4TPU project proposal (October 2026), the following specific support types are requested from partners:

✓Financial support for programme implementation
✓Medical and health education supplies
✓Equipment and outreach materials
✓Staff training and technical assistance
✓Support for youth peer education
✓M&E support and evaluation
✓Referral and specialist service partnerships
