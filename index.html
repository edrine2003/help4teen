Who We Are

We Exist Because Young Parents Deserve Support.

Hope 4 Teen Parents Uganda (H4TPU) is a community-based health organisation at Mella Trading Center, Tororo District. We bring essential health information, preventive services, counselling, referrals and community support closer to adolescents, teenage mothers, teenage fathers and their children.

We recognise that teenage parents need more than a clinic visit. They need accessible information, respectful services, early prevention, timely referral, psychosocial support, parenting skills and community partnership — all in one place they can trust.

Our Foundation

What We Stand For

Every action we take is guided by these three pillars.

🎯
Mission

Our Mission

To provide accessible, respectful and community-based health services, education, prevention, referral and supportive care — with particular attention to adolescents, young people and teenage parents.

🔭
Vision

Our Vision

Healthy, informed and empowered young people and teenage parents who can make responsible decisions, access timely health care and build healthier futures for themselves and their children.

🤝
Promise

Our Promise

We listen without judging. We provide accurate information. We protect your privacy. And when you need medical care, we connect you with qualified health workers who can help.

Your Donation Reaches a Young Parent Who Has Nowhere Else to Turn.

Right now, there are teenage mothers in Mella and surrounding communities who are pregnant, frightened, and have no trusted person to ask for health information. There are young fathers who want to do the right thing but don't know how.

Your donation — however small — helps us reach them. It funds community sessions, health information materials, and the people who show up for young parents every single day.
